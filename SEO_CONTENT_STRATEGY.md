# 📝 SEO Content Strategy & Writing Guide

## 🎯 Content Writing Guidelines

### Keyword Density Target
- **Primary Keywords:** ~1.5% density
- **Secondary Keywords:** ~0.5-1% density
- **Long-tail Keywords:** Natural integration
- **Location Keywords:** "Chennai" mentioned 3-5 times per page

### Readability Target
- **Grade Level:** 6-8 (Easy to read)
- **Sentence Length:** 15-20 words average
- **Paragraph Length:** 3-4 sentences
- **Use simple, clear language**

### Content Length Targets
- **Homepage:** 800-1200 words
- **Service Pages:** 500-800 words
- **Product Pages:** 300-500 words
- **Category Pages:** 400-600 words

## 📝 Content Writing Tips

1. **Write for Humans First:** Content should be helpful and readable
2. **Natural Keyword Integration:** Don't stuff keywords unnaturally
3. **Use Location Naturally:** Mention Chennai and Tamil Nadu naturally
4. **Include CTAs:** Every page should have a clear call-to-action
5. **Answer Questions:** Address common customer questions
6. **Use Lists:** Bullet points and numbered lists improve readability
7. **Short Paragraphs:** Keep paragraphs to 3-4 sentences
8. **Simple Language:** Use Grade 6-8 reading level
9. **Be Specific:** Include specific details, numbers, and facts
10. **Update Regularly:** Keep content fresh and up-to-date
